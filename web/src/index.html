Configs:

Live Control

Sliders animate over 1 s. Pauses polling for a control while you're dragging it.

Reports

Saved reports:
Reports are generated after a config session. Where they're stored is configured in Settings; they're always uploaded to the generator when its URL is set.

Device Settings

Hardware settings (pins, codec, LED) take effect after a reboot. Generator URL and default volume are seeds applied at next boot.
